Finasteride stops male pattern hair loss

Large randomised trials support finasteride for androgenetic alopecia. The evidence is solid; the decision is still a medical one, not a forum one.

Evidence

  • Randomised, placebo-controlled trials in men with androgenetic alopecia. Kaufman et al., J Am Acad Dermatol 1998 (rct)

What it costs you

Reversibility
gains reverse within about 12 months of stopping
Cost
$10-30/month
Time to effect
6-12 months
Harm reduction: A minority report sexual side effects, and whether symptoms can persist after stopping is genuinely contested rather than settled. That uncertainty is the reason this belongs in a conversation with a doctor who knows your history. Not to be handled by women who are or may become pregnant.
