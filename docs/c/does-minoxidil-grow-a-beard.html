Minoxidil grows a beard

Two small randomised trials — one in men, one in transmen — found modest density increases from topical minoxidil on the beard area. Real, replicated, and far smaller than the results the internet attaches to it.

Evidence

  • Randomised, double-masked, placebo-controlled trial of 3% minoxidil lotion for beard enhancement. Ingprasert et al., Journal of Dermatology 2016 (small-rct)
  • Second randomised trial of topical 3% minoxidil for facial hair enhancement, in transmen. Randomised trial, Journal of Dermatological Treatment (small-rct)

What it costs you

Reversibility
gains fade after stopping
Cost
$10-20/month, indefinitely
Time to effect
16+ weeks
Harm reduction: Two small trials with modest effects, not the transformations sold on social media. Oral minoxidil for cosmetic beard growth is off-label and has cardiovascular effects — that is a prescribing decision, not a grooming purchase.
