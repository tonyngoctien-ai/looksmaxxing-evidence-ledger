Bone smashing remodels your facial structure

Striking your own face to 'remodel' bone is a misreading of Wolff's law. Bone adapts to gradual loading, not to trauma — and facial fractures heal asymmetrically.

Evidence

  • Wolff's law describes bone adaptation to sustained mechanical loading, not to acute injury; facial trauma outcomes are documented in the maxillofacial fracture literature. Maxillofacial fracture outcome literature (mechanistic-misreading)
