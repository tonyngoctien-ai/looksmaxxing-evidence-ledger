Facial exercises make you look younger

A single 27-person study without a control group reported modest mid-face improvement after 20 weeks. That is a hint worth following up, not a result worth selling a programme on.

Evidence

  • Uncontrolled 20-week facial exercise study, n=27, raters aware of the timeline. Alam et al., JAMA Dermatology 2018 (small-uncontrolled)
