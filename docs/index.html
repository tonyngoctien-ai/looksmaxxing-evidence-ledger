SupportedlifestyleSleep changes how attractive your face looksIn a controlled experiment, the same faces photographed after sleep deprivation were rated less healthy and less attractive by observers who knew nothing about the sleep condition. Free, fast, and the least discussed item in this niche. SupportedskinDaily sunscreen prevents skin agingA 4.5-year randomised trial found measurably less skin aging in the daily-sunscreen group than in the discretionary-use group. This is the best-evidenced entry on this list. SupportedskinTretinoin improves skin texture and photoagingDouble-blind vehicle-controlled trials have shown improvement in photoaged skin. It is one of very few topicals with this level of evidence behind it. SupportedfitnessLosing body fat changes your facial definitionThis is the honest answer sitting underneath most jawline questions. Facial adiposity tracks body composition, and it moves facial definition further than any posture habit or exercise gadget. SupportedhairFinasteride stops male pattern hair lossLarge randomised trials support finasteride for androgenetic alopecia. The evidence is solid; the decision is still a medical one, not a forum one. MixedhairMinoxidil grows a beardTwo small randomised trials — one in men, one in transmen — found modest density increases from topical minoxidil on the beard area. Real, replicated, and far smaller than the results the internet attaches to it. MixedfitnessHard chewing gum builds your jawlineHeavy chewing can genuinely enlarge the masseter — but that is muscle, not bone, and it widens the rear of the jaw rather than sharpening the front. Masseter hypertrophy is something other people pay to have reduced. MixedskinFacial exercises make you look youngerA single 27-person study without a control group reported modest mid-face improvement after 20 weeks. That is a hint worth following up, not a result worth selling a programme on. MixedskinCollagen supplements improve your skinSeveral small trials report modest elasticity improvements. Most were funded by companies selling collagen, which is not disqualifying but is exactly the context you need before reading the effect size. UnsupportedfitnessMewing permanently changes your jawlineNo controlled study has measured adult facial bone remodelling from tongue posture. The circulating before-and-afters are dominated by body-fat change, posture, camera angle and lighting. HarmfulfitnessSARMs are a safer alternative to steroidsThey do affect body composition. They are also unapproved for human use, and chemical analysis of products sold as SARMs found that a large share were mislabelled or contained something else entirely. HarmfulsurgeryBone smashing remodels your facial structureStriking your own face to 'remodel' bone is a misreading of Wolff's law. Bone adapts to gradual loading, not to trauma — and facial fractures heal asymmetrically. HarmfulsurgeryLimb lengthening surgery makes you tallerIt works — the procedure genuinely adds height. It is also among the most invasive elective operations available, with recovery measured in years, and it is marketed to young men in ways that badly understate that.
